Our Orchards

We name the farm on every label.

Most dry fruit changes hands six times before it's packed — trader to trader, warehouse to warehouse — and the origin gets lost along the way. We buy in person, from the same eight orchards each season, and we write their names on the jar. If a batch doesn't meet our tasting panel, it doesn't ship. That's the whole quality system.
